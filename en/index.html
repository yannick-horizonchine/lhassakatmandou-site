Lhasa to Kathmandu by Bike

18 September to 7 October 2027 — around twelve days in the saddle, around ten passes above 4,000m and about 9,000m of climbing, from the Potala Palace to Everest Base Camp, then across the Himalaya to Kathmandu.

Tibet → Nepal 18 Sep → 7 Oct 2027

This is not a simple crossing — it's a cycling journey across one of the highest, most spectacular plateaus on Earth. From Lhasa and the Potala Palace, around twelve days of riding along the Friendship Highway cross around ten passes above 4,000m — several of them above 5,000m, including the Gyagtso La at 5,220m — for about 9,000m of climbing. Along the way: Yamdrok Lake, the towns of Gyantse and Shigatse, Everest Base Camp at the foot of the north face, then the crossing of the Himalaya into Nepal and the temples of the Kathmandu valley. A demanding route, for trained cyclists who are at ease in the mountains.

Two days to discover Durbar Square, Patan, Bhaktapur and the stupas of Swayambhunath and Boudhanath.

Altitude acclimatisation required beforehand

This trip starts high and climbs fast: around twelve riding days between 3,400 and over 5,200m, with stages that can exceed 120km and 1,300m of ascent. Good acclimatisation is essential — the first days (Xining, then Lhasa) allow a gentle climb before the passes of the plateau, and Axel, your guide, is a medical correspondent of IFREMMONT: he can contact an altitude-medicine specialist at any time. Oxygen, a portable hyperbaric chamber (PAC) and a full first-aid kit also travel with the team throughout the trip. As with our other very high-altitude routes, we can help you prepare your acclimatisation before departure.

Your Guide

Axel, Guide and Explorer

Your guide for the whole trip

Axel

Axel is a professional adventurer and outdoor leader. He has worked in demanding environments — the Alps, the Amazon, the Sahara — and has completed high-mountain expeditions, a dugout canoe crossing and desert expeditions. This experience has given him a deep, practical knowledge of life in the wild.

A former environmental and energy engineer, he worked at the Shanghai Astronomical Observatory. Drawn to Chinese history and culture, he studied Chinese and ancient Chinese history. Since 2024, he has been leading travellers on long-distance crossings in Europe, China and other countries. He has already guided in Tibet and has crossed China by bike.

He loves mountain adventure and wildlife watching, and enjoys sharing the cultural stories of the road with the group.

Languages: Chinese, English, French, Spanish
Health: medical correspondent of IFREMMONT (Institut de formation et de recherche en médecine de montagne)

Day by Day

Full Itinerary

DAY 1 · 18 September

Arrival in Xining (2300m)

Arrival in Xining, the largest city on the Tibetan plateau. Welcome on arrival and transfer to your accommodation.

Overnight: Xining, 2300m
DAY 2 · 19 September

Xining → Lhasa

Full-day visit of Kumbum Monastery, built on the birthplace of Tsongkhapa and where the explorer Alexandra David-Neel once stayed for three years. Boarding in the evening on the “Tibet train”, the highest in the world: around 22 hours on board for a scenic and gentle arrival on the roof of the world.

Transport: Overnight train
Overnight: On board the train
DAY 3 · 20 September

Arrival in Lhasa (3835m)

Evening arrival in Lhasa. Welcome by your guide and transfer to your hotel.

Overnight: Lhasa, 3835m
DAY 4 · 21 September

Lhasa by bike

Cycling tour of the city and its surroundings: group photo in front of the Potala, the most imposing building in Lhasa and former seat of the Tibetan government, followed by a visit to Sera and Drepung monasteries, 8km from the city centre – the largest monastic complex in Tibet.

Overnight: Lhasa, 3835m
DAY 5 · 22 September

Lhasa

Morning visit of Jokhang Monastery, built in 647 when Buddhism was introduced to Tibet – the cradle and sacred home of Tibetan Buddhism. Free time afterwards to wander the lively Barkhor street surrounding it.

Overnight: Lhasa, 3835m
DAY 6 · 23 September

Lhasa → Quxu (3600m)

Departure towards the Chushue area, following the Lhasa river, the main tributary of the Yarlung Tsangpo (Brahmaputra). Possible stop at a Buddhist mural and at Nyethang Drolma Lhakang temple, detour via Chushue bridge. Camp near Quxu.

Cycling: 85km
Elevation: +370m / −400m
Overnight: Camp near Quxu, 3600m
DAY 7 · 24 September

Quxu → Nagarze (4410m)

Crossing of Khampa La pass (4795m), with a beautiful view over the Yarlung valley, the Yarlung Tsangpo river and Yamdrok Lake. Descent along the lake shore for several kilometres. Camp near the lake and Nagarze.

Cycling: 59km
Elevation: +1390m / −560m
Overnight: Camp near Nagarze, 4410m
DAY 8 · 25 September

Nagarze → Gyantse (4020m)

Crossing of a new pass, the Karo La, at 5045m. The road climbs gently up the valley before a steeper final stretch. The arrival at the pass is spectacular, with a 7000m+ peak on one side and a 6400m mountain on the other. The afternoon descent is long but very rideable, with a glimpse of Gyantse – a very typical town with its fortress perched on a promontory overlooking it. Hotel night.

Cycling: 122km
Elevation: +1115m / −1140m
Overnight: Gyantse, 4020m
DAY 9 · 26 September

Gyantse → Shigatse (3835m)

Ride this morning towards Shigatse, Tibet's second city. Home to Tashilhunpo Monastery, the former official seat of the Panchen Lama, Tibet's second political and spiritual authority. It houses the world's largest bronze statue, built in 1904 for Maitreya, the Buddha of the future. Hotel night.

Cycling: 94km
Elevation: +140m / −320m
Overnight: Shigatse, 3835m
DAY 10 · 27 September

Shigatse → Jingdingzhen (3905m)

Riding day on the Friendship Highway, crossing two fairly easy small passes. Camp night.

Cycling: 98km
Elevation: +820m / −470m
Overnight: Camp at Jingdingzhen, 3905m
DAY 11 · 28 September

Jingdingzhen → Lhatse (4050m)

Crossing of Yulung La pass (4450m), then a descent through superb Tibetan high-plateau scenery down to Lhatse. Hotel night.

Cycling: 52km
Elevation: +440m / −610m
Overnight: Lhatse, 4050m
DAY 12 · 29 September

Lhatse → Shegar (4320m)

The highest pass of the whole trip awaits: Gyagtso La, at 5220m, then a descent into the valley. First views of Everest, weather permitting. Hotel night.

Cycling: 81km
Elevation: +1300m / −1000m
Overnight: Shegar, 4320m
DAY 13 · 30 September

Shegar → Tashi Dzom → Rongbuk (5000m)

Crossing of Pang La pass (5195m). From the top, the Himalayan range unfolds breathtakingly, with no fewer than five 8000m+ peaks in front of you. Descent to Tashi Dzom village, then transfer by vehicle to Rongbuk Monastery, the highest monastery in the world, near Everest Base Camp. Guesthouse night.

Cycling: 62km
Elevation: +970m / −1130m
Transport: Bus, Tashi Dzom → Rongbuk
Overnight: Rongbuk, 5000m
DAY 14 · 1 October

Rongbuk → Tashi Dzom → Tingri (4350m)

A final pass at 4915m, the Namar La, through a wild and little-used valley, followed by a fast descent to the Tingri plain. Hotel night.

Cycling: 59km
Elevation: +800m / −630m
Overnight: Tingri, 4350m
DAY 15 · 2 October

Tingri → Nyalam Tongla (4800m)

Back on the Friendship Highway, continuing to Nyalam Tongla pass. The climb is long and the final flat stretch will wear more than one rider down. Fast descent, then a camp night between the two passes, at 4800m altitude.

Cycling: 88km
Elevation: +920m / −390m
Overnight: Camp at Nyalam Tongla, 4800m
DAY 16 · 3 October

Nyalam Tongla → Nyalam (3400m)

Crossing of the last pass of the trip, Yagru Xong La (5125m) – also the last viewpoint over the Himalayan range, with Xishapangma, an 8000m+ peak, visible on your right. Easy and spectacular descent, then flatter riding down the valley. Hotel night.

Cycling: 64km
Elevation: +460m / −1560m
Overnight: Nyalam, 3400m
DAY 17 · 4 October

Nyalam → Kathmandu (1350m)

Descent by vehicle towards the border post, through magnificent scenery. Border crossing and Nepalese visa formalities, meeting with the Nepalese team, then drive to Kathmandu. Hotel night.

Transport: Bus, 30km
Overnight: Kathmandu, 1350m
DAY 18 · 5 October

Kathmandu (1350m)

After breakfast, visit Durbar Square then Patan, one of the valley's three ancient royal cities, mainly Buddhist and renowned for its bronze-casting artisans. Continue to Swayambhunath, a hilltop temple home to monkeys and a huge Buddhist stupa overlooking the Kathmandu valley. Hotel night.

Overnight: Kathmandu, 1350m
DAY 19 · 6 October

Kathmandu (1350m)

Morning visit of Bhaktapur, a sumptuous pedestrian city 15km from Kathmandu, the most beautiful of the valley's three royal cities. Continue to Pashupatinath temple and Boudhanath stupa. Hotel night.

Overnight: Kathmandu, 1350m
DAY 20 · 7 October

Kathmandu, end of the trip

Breakfast, then transfer to Tribhuvan International Airport. End of our services.

Transport: Airport transfer
